Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 20, 2026
We believe privacy is a fundamental right. aSoft apps are designed to collect as little data as possible — and never sell your data to anyone.

Apps covered by this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to all applications developed and published by Anton Stremovskyi (aSoft).

Data we do NOT collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Your name, email address, or other personal identifiers (unless you contact us directly)
  • Your location data
  • Your contacts, photos, or files (unless an app explicitly needs local access for its core feature — in that case, processing stays on your device)
  • Your browsing history
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA) for cross-app tracking

Analytics

Some of our apps may use privacy-focused analytics to understand how features are used and to improve the product. This applies only to apps where analytics is enabled — not every app collects this data.

When used, analytics may include anonymous product interaction events, such as:

  • That the app was opened
  • Which features or screens were used
  • That a core action was completed (e.g. a calculation, scan, or purchase flow — without the underlying content)

We use providers that prioritize privacy (e.g. Aptabase). Analytics data is used only for product improvement, is not linked to your identity, is not used for advertising, is not shared with data brokers, and is not sold. These analytics do not use the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) and do not require Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission.

Each app's App Store privacy label describes exactly what that app collects. If an app does not list analytics data, it does not collect it.

Data stored on your device

Apps may store preferences, settings, progress, or content locally on your device using standard iOS/macOS mechanisms (UserDefaults, Keychain, local files). This data is used to provide app functionality.

Unless an app explicitly offers optional cloud sync (see below), this data stays on your device.

iCloud sync

Some apps optionally sync data via your personal iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit framework. This data is stored in your private iCloud storage and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. We have no access to this data.

On-device processing

Some apps process data entirely on your device — for example local calculations, game logic, motion input, or on-device AI. In those cases, the relevant data is not sent to aSoft servers for processing or storage.

Game Center

Apps that integrate with Apple Game Center submit scores and achievement progress as required by that feature. Game Center data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

In-App Purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple via the App Store or by Lemon Squeezy for direct sales. We do not receive or store your payment card details. For Lemon Squeezy purchases, their Privacy Policy applies.

We may receive confirmation that a purchase was made (via StoreKit or equivalent) to unlock features on your device.

Advertising (future versions)

Some apps may display ads provided by third-party networks (e.g. Google AdMob). Those networks may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve ads, subject to your device's privacy settings. Where offered, a paid version may remove ads.

Children's privacy

Our apps do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will delete it immediately.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of our apps after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
